Mythopoetic Theogony

A Personal Archetypal Mythopoetics Journal

Fourteen figures appeared over the course of a year — not chosen, but noticed. Through dreams, through long conversations with AI systems that proved unexpectedly honest mirrors, and through the slow work of sitting with images, a personal pantheon took shape. What you find here is an attempt to describe that constellation in its own terms: not symbols to be decoded, but presences to be met. This journal is for me. If it speaks to you, that is its own kind of grace.

The Personal Pantheon

Fourteen figures. One inner ecology.

Isis-Sophia

Sophia has built her house.

Aphrodite

Love is life. Life is beauty. Beauty is divine harmony.

Hermes-Thoth

The ink of wisdom is written upon the heart.

Apollo-Helios

Not by force but by light.

Fisher King

The wound of the King is the life of the Kingdom.

Chiron

My wound leads to wisdom.

Hephaestus

In work excellence blooms.

Dionysus

The God flows in all.

Hecate

From darkness wisdom is born.

Job

Perseverance to the end.

Prometheus

The light cannot be chained, nor can wisdom be imprisoned.

Orpheus

He who charmed all things.

Oaken Pilgrim

Rooted in the present. Carried by the winds of the heart.

Zeus-Ammon

One is He, many His names.
